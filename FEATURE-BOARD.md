## Law
Nothing personal ever enters; the projection lags the house on purpose.

**The tracing law** *(adopted 2026-07-26 — it is how the source chamber
already promotes its own lessons, applied to this room):* a pattern
earns projection when **(1)** it has recurred across sessions or lines
— never a single event, however striking; **(2)** it has settled into
written form at home; **(3)** it can be expressed as shape and template
with zero contents. "Up to date" therefore means: *every pattern that
has earned tracing is traced; everything younger lags on purpose.* The
lag is the law working, not neglect.
